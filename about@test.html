About the Event

The Annual Interuniversity Debate Competition is a prestigious event that brings together students from various universities to showcase their debating skills. Participants will have the opportunity to engage in thought-provoking discussions, present their arguments, and demonstrate their ability to think critically and articulate their ideas effectively. The competition aims to foster a spirit of healthy debate, encourage intellectual growth, and promote collaboration among students from different academic backgrounds.The event will feature a series of rounds, including preliminary debates, semi-finals, and the grand finale, where the best teams will compete for the championship title. Judges will evaluate participants based on their clarity of expression, logical reasoning, and overall presentation. We invite all students to participate and make the most of this exciting opportunity to enhance their public speaking and debating skills.
